# Investigate sweep: should an exposure undo the theft?

3000 games per row, same seeds 1..3000 in every row, current rules (game.json) plus the rule in the row, 15 players, 10 nights, no wilderness events. ± is the 95% margin. Rules: A today (theft stands, thief pays the investigator 1); B the loot is taken back, half (rounded up) to the victim and the rest to the investigator, plus the bounty 1; C all loot back to the victim, bounty 1; D all loot back, bounty 2. The thief pays loot first, then the bounty, only from what they hold. AI today = players ignore the new payouts; AI weighs payout = investigating is scaled by what an exposure pays the investigator (relative to today's 1) and an exposed thief turns cautious. Ranges 7/3/1 + human memory: well fed 7+, getting by 3-6, starving 1-2, bandit; players follow 3 others closely. Steals landed = took something at the time; landed and kept = landed minus those an exposure took back. Detective and shark columns are a multiple of a fair share (1.00 = fair).
