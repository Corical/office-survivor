A 2.5-minute animated explainer that teaches Survivors to someone who has never played a game like it. One idea per scene, big numbers on screen, no jargon, and captions throughout for people watching on mute.

## Full narration

The voiceover in one piece: 376 words, about 2 minutes 35 seconds at a calm pace. One paragraph per scene, so a pause between paragraphs lines up with a scene change.

For the next two weeks, the office becomes a survival game. Food is scarce, everyone is hungry, and anyone could turn on you.

The game runs for ten workdays. Your goal: stay alive, and on the last night, grab one of five seats on the rescue helicopter.

Everyone starts with seven rations. Every night, you eat one. Run out, and you become a bandit. More on that later.

Each day, you pick one secret move in Discord before midnight. Nobody else can see what you chose. You have five options.

Scavenge. Go looking for food and find nothing, one or two rations. If you forget to play, you scavenge automatically.

Steal. Pick someone and take two of their rations. They'll know they were robbed. They won't know it was you.

Guard. You're protected tonight and tomorrow night. Anyone who tries to rob you gets caught, named in the news, and pays you two rations. You only get three guards, so choose your moment.

Investigate. Spy on one person and find out what they did that night. Catch them stealing, and they're exposed and pay you one ration.

Gift. Give some of your food to someone else. Only the two of you will know.

Every morning at half past seven, the news drops in Discord: who got robbed, who got caught, and who ran out of food. You'll always know what happened. You'll rarely know who did it.

Run out of food and you're a bandit. Bandits can only steal, but get back to three rations and you rejoin the survivors.

You'll also get a secret goal that only you know about. And once a day you can post an anonymous message on the radio channel. Tips, accusations, lies. Your call.

On the last night, the helicopter lands. The five survivors with the most food get a seat. A seat and your secret goal both earn points, and the most points wins. Then comes the big reveal: every theft and every secret, out in the open.

It takes two minutes a day, it's completely optional, and nothing in the game touches real work or real money. Type slash join in Discord and you'll get a secret alias, so nobody knows who's who. And remember: trust no one.

## Look and sound

Keep it simple and consistent: if every action always has the same icon and colour, people learn the game from the pictures alone.

| Action | Icon | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scavenge | Shovel | Green |
| Steal | Grabbing hand | Red |
| Guard | Shield | Blue |
| Investigate | Magnifying glass | Amber |
| Gift | Gift box | Purple |

- **Style:** flat, friendly cartoon. Players are simple avatars, food is a tin can, and night scenes are dark blue rather than scary.
- **On-screen text:** one short line per scene, big, with the numbers in it. Never more than about eight words.
- **Captions:** burned in for the whole video, since many people will watch on mute.
- **Narrator:** calm and slightly dramatic, like a late-night radio host. Not a hype voice.
- **Music:** low, tense background beat that drops out for the reveal in scene 13.
- **Format:** 16:9 for sharing in Discord and meetings. A 9:16 phone cut works if the on-screen text stays centred.

## Production

The video is built by an agent driving the Craft apps over MCP, following `video/AGENT.md` in the survivors kit. Each stage saves files the next one picks up.

| Stage | Tool | Makes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Voiceover | A person, or text-to-speech | One recording per scene |
| 2. Assets | VectorCraft | Characters, props and the five action icons |
| 3. Chat screens | FigCraft | The phone, #news, #radio and /join screens |
| 4. Scenes | EffectCraft | One animated clip per scene |
| 5. Audio mix | SoundCraft or FilmCraft | Voice, music and sound effects in one track |
| 6. Final | FilmCraft | The finished video with captions |

Every number in the script comes from one rules file, `game.json`, which the simulator also reads. Change a rule there and run `video/build.py`: it rewrites the script, screens and captions, and lists only the recordings, drawings and scenes that need redoing. This doc is written by hand, so update it when the rules change.
