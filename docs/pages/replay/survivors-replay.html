What players can see

Nobody ever sees anyone else's rations. Everything a player knows about others comes from #news, #radio and their own private replies, so "who looks richest" is always a guess. The "Others' best guess" column and the dashed chart line work that guess out the way the simulated players do: 7 to start, plus or minus every change #news revealed. A nightly meal and an average scavenge cancel out, so neither is counted; anyone not announced as a bandit is assumed to have at least 1. Scavenging luck and gifts never show up in it, which is where the gap to the truth comes from.
